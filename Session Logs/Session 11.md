Ras has a brief conversation with him, but it doesn't amount to much before Beehan interrupts them.
Beehan introduces the newcomer as Steev -- Corporal Steev Polpatine.
He describes him as a kind of auditor to help improve relations with Whykin -- a minder, essentially. He will be following the group around and giving them a hand until further notice.
Soon, a disturbance in the force alerts Ras that something is going on with the leyline.
We find a D-Bee woman outside appearing to be in intense pain from the strange leyline activity.
Deciding to investigate right away, we come to a Nexus Point in the leyline where a large demon is summoned into this world.
* A psychic ripples passes through the world as it emerges.

* A fierce battle ensues.
